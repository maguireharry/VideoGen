# Project 01: Nano Banana 2 Comedy Animation 🍌⚡

An animated cartoon short created with **Nano Banana 2 (Google Gemini 3.1 Flash Image)**, animated at 20 frames per second over 30 seconds (600 frames total).

## Video Specifications
- **Duration**: 30.00 seconds
- **Frame Rate**: 20 FPS (Frames Per Second)
- **Total Frame Count**: 600 unique rendered frames (`frame_0001.png` to `frame_0600.png`)
- **Resolution**: 1280 x 720 (720p HD Widescreen)
- **Video Codec**: H.264 / AVC (CRF 18, YUV420p)
- **Audio Codec**: AAC Stereo 44.1 kHz
- **Audio Track**: Slapstick SFX & cartoon bouncy walking bassline (slide whistle, boings, sirens, splats, rocket thrusters, laser zaps)

## Storyboard
1. **Frames 001 - 060**: *Awakening* — Nano Banana wakes up, puts on pixel sunglasses.
2. **Frames 061 - 120**: *Fruit Bowl Escape* — Toothpick pole-vault over grumpy apples.
3. **Frames 121 - 180**: *Blender of Doom* — Terrifying blender roars to life.
4. **Frames 181 - 240**: *Tactical Slide* — Action movie counter slide under blades.
5. **Frames 241 - 300**: *Chef Monkey Ambush* — Sneaky monkey thwarted by tactical peels.
6. **Frames 301 - 360**: *Monkey Wipeout* — 720° spin into fluffy whipped cream!
7. **Frames 361 - 420**: *Supercharged* — 9000mg potassium electric overload!
8. **Frames 421 - 480**: *Rocket Launch* — NANO-X bottle rocket blasts into orbit.
9. **Frames 481 - 540**: *Space Disco* — Zero-G disco dance in orbit.
10. **Frames 541 - 600**: *Splat!* — Epic hero return slips on its own peel.

# Shivamogga Kannada Drama : ಶಿವಮೊಗ್ಗ ನಾಟಕ 🎭 : 600-Frame 35mm Cinema Film
*(Tunga River Heritage, Western Ghats Monsoon & Rangamandira Passion)*

Inspired by legendary Kannada theatre culture (Girish Karnad, B.V. Karanth, Rangayana) and the lush Malnad geography of Shivamogga, Karnataka.

## 🎵 Procedural Soundtrack Design (`scripts/generate_soundtrack.py`)
- **Raag Bhoopali / Mohanam Mode**: S R G P D S' (C, D, E, G, A) evoking profound bhakti, longing, and redemption.
- **Microtonal Bansuri (Bamboo Flute)**: Breath noise, meend slides, and emotional vibrato.
- **Acoustic Tanpura Drone**: Pa-Sa-Sa-Sa acoustic harmonics (G3, C4, C4, C3).
- **Clay Pot Ghata & Tabla Percussion**: Deep resonance and traditional Malnad rhythmic cadence.
- **Deep Brass Temple Bells**: High-resonance brass bells ringing at dawn and emotional climax.
- **Ambient Western Ghats Rain**: Natural monsoon downpour and thunder foley.

## 🛠️ Video Pipeline (`scripts/render_animation_frames.py` & `scripts/compile_video.py`)
- **Frames**: 600 individual rendered PNG frames (`frames/frame_0001.png` - `frames/frame_0600.png`)
- **Parallel Render**: Multi-core rendering across 16 CPU cores via `multiprocessing.Pool`
- **Output Video**: `output/shivamogga_kannada_drama.mp4` (1280x720 Progressive, H.264 CRF 18, 24 FPS, AAC audio)
- **Preview GIF**: `output/shivamogga_kannada_drama_preview.gif` (Lanczos palette generation)

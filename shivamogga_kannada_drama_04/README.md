## 🎵 Procedural Soundtrack Design (`scripts/generate_soundtrack.py`)
- **Raag Bhoopali / Mohanam** melodic scale tuned to C# (Sa = 138.59 Hz).
- **Tanpura Drone**: Resonant multi-harmonic cyclical drone establishing classical Indian atmosphere.
- **Bansuri (Bamboo Flute)**: Micro-tonal pitch glides, breath turbulence, and expressive vibrato.
- **Tala Roopaka Tabla Rhythm**: Deep modulated Bayan (*Dagga*) pitch bending strokes accompanied by crisp Dayan (*Na/Tin*) resonance.
- **Temple Bell & River Ambience**: Authentic bronze bell harmonics (1,108 Hz) layered over ambient Western Ghats mountain river flow.
- **Cinematic Strings**: Rich multi-octave string pad swelling during the emotional Jog Falls climax and banyan tree reunion.

## 🛠️ Video Pipeline (`scripts/compile_video.py`)
- **Resolution**: 1280x720 Progressive (16:9 Widescreen) @ 25 FPS
- **Cinematography**: Dynamic subpixel Ken Burns pan/zoom camera motions tailored to each emotional beat.
- **Transitions**: 15-frame optical cross-dissolves between scenes.
- **Typography**: Dual-script Kannada & English cinematic title cards with soft shadow drop and subtle letterboxing.
- **Audio Codec**: AAC stereo @ 192 kbps.
